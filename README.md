<p align="center"><b>Voice AI interviews for auto dealers across the US and Canada</b></p>

This repository publishes the Cenivoz company profile at **[cenivoz.github.io](https://cenivoz.github.io/)**.

Cenivoz gives every dealership applicant a spoken first interview, over the phone or in a browser, at whatever time works for them. Managers then review each interview's scores, recommendation, written transcript and recording, and choose who to meet. Pay, offers and hiring decisions always stay with the dealership.
